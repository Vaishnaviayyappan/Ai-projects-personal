**Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

**Project Name:** Voice-First AI

**Tagline:** "Elevate your experience with voice-first interactions"

**Overview:**
Voice-First AI is a SaaS platform that leverages natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML) to provide a seamless, voice-centric experience for users. Our goal is to capitalize on the growing trend of voice assistants and mobile apps that prioritize voice interactions.

**Target Users:**

* **Primary Audience:** Busy professionals, parents, and individuals with mobility or dexterity issues who struggle with typing.
* **Secondary Audience:** Small business owners, entrepreneurs, and marketers seeking to optimize their customer engagement and conversion rates through voice-based experiences.

**Market:**
The global voice-activated assistant market is projected to reach $10.2 billion by 2025, growing at a CAGR of 20.7% (Source: MarketsandMarkets). Our target audience is among the most valuable consumers who are willing to adopt innovative technologies that simplify their lives.

**Business Model:**
We will generate revenue through:

* **Subscription-based model:** Offer a tiered pricing structure for individuals and businesses, with discounts for annual commitments.
* **Advertising:** Partner with relevant brands to display targeted, voice-activated ads within our platform.
* **Enterprise solutions:** Provide customized voice-first AI solutions for large corporations and organizations.

**Resources:**

* **Development Team:** Hire a team of experienced NLP engineers, ML researchers, and front-end developers specializing in Next.js, Supabase, and PostgreSQL.
* **Hardware and Infrastructure:** Invest in high-performance servers, cloud storage, and scalable infrastructure to ensure seamless voice-activated interactions.
* **Partnerships:** Collaborate with leading voice assistant vendors (e.g., Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant) to integrate our platform with their APIs.

**Goal:**
Launch a minimum viable product (MVP) that integrates phone-call AI, WhatsApp voice support, and basic form filling within the first 6 months. Expand features to include voice navigation, advanced NLP capabilities, and multi-tenant architecture within the next 12 months.

**Key Features:**

1. **Phone-Call AI:** Integrate with popular phone service providers (e.g., Twilio) to enable users to make hands-free calls via our platform.
2. **WhatsApp Voice Support:** Leverage WhatsApp's API to provide voice-based support for users, allowing them to communicate through voice messages and audio calls.
3. **Voice-Based Form Filling:** Develop an AI-powered form filling system that uses NLP to detect user input and fill out relevant fields.
4. **Voice Navigation:** Create a voice-guided experience that allows users to navigate our platform using voice commands.

**Technical Requirements:**

1. **Next.js and Supabase:** Utilize these technologies for building the frontend and backend of our application, respectively.
2. **PostgreSQL and LangGraph:** Leverage these databases to store user data and integrate with AI algorithms.
3. **Claude/GPT:** Implement this language generation model to power our NLP capabilities.
4. **Vercel and Stripe:** Use these platforms for hosting and payment processing, respectively.

**Security and Scalability:**
Implement robust security measures to protect user data, including encryption, access controls, and regular security audits. Ensure scalability by using load balancing, caching, and containerization techniques.

This PRD provides a solid foundation for our Voice-First AI project. By prioritizing these features and technical requirements, we can deliver a high-quality, voice-centric experience that sets us apart from competitors in the market.
