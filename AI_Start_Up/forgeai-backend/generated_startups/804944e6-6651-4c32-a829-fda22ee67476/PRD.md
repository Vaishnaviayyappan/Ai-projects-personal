**Idea: AI Meal Planner for Students**

**Target Users:**

* Students (primary) - high school, college, and university students
* Parents (secondary) - parents of students who want to support their child's healthy eating habits
* Meal planning services (secondary) - meal kit delivery services, food delivery platforms, and school cafeterias

**Market:**

* Demographics:
	+ Age: 18-30
	+ Location: Urban and suburban areas
	+ Interests: Health, wellness, food, and technology
* Psychographics:
	+ Values convenience, sustainability, and health
	+ Interested in trying new recipes and flavors
	+ Willing to pay for premium features and services
* Market size: Estimated 10 million students in the US alone

**Business Model:**

* Subscription-based model:
	+ Basic: $9.99/month (limited features, recipe access)
	+ Premium: $19.99/month (full features, personalized meal planning, meal kit integrations)
	+ Pro: $49.99/month (additional features, priority customer support, and meal planning for large groups)
* Advertising revenue:
	+ Display ads on the app
	+ Sponsored content and product placements
* Partnerships:
	+ Meal kit delivery services
	+ Food delivery platforms
	+ School cafeterias

**Resources:**

* Development team:
	+ 2-3 front-end developers
	+ 1-2 back-end developers
	+ 1-2 designers
* Infrastructure:
	+ Cloud hosting (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud)
	+ Database management (e.g., PostgreSQL)
	+ APIs and integrations (e.g., Stripe, Supabase)
* Marketing budget:
	+ Social media advertising
	+ Influencer partnerships
	+ Content marketing

**Goal:**

* Create a user-friendly, AI-powered meal planner app that provides personalized meal planning, recipe suggestions, and grocery lists
* Achieve 10,000 downloads within the first six months of launch
* Generate $1 million in revenue within the first year
* Expand to 100,000 users within the first two years
