## 0:00 - The Hook (Problem Statement)
**Action**: Start with the **Home Screen** open on Mobile/Web.
**Script**: 
>"Agriculture feeds the world, but our farmers are starving for information. They face pest attacks, volatile prices, and isolation—often leading to distress. Existing apps are fragmented and complex. Today, we introduce **Farm Connect**: A unified, AI-powered lifeline for the modern farmer."

## 0:30 - Core Experience (UI & Design)
**Action**: Scroll smoothly through the **Hero Section**. Tap a card to show the animation.
**Script**:
>"We built Farm Connect with a 'Nature-First' design system. It uses smooth, organic animations (powered by Reanimated) to ensure even non-tech-savvy farmers feel at ease. It's not just an app; it's a digital ecosystem. Notice the glassmorphism and breathing motion—it feels premium yet grounded."

## 1:00 - The AI Innovation (Multi-Agent System)
**Action**: Navigate to **"AI Expert"** (Chat). Type: *"My wheat leaves are turning yellow."*
**Script**:
>"This isn't just a chatbot. It's a **LangGraph Multi-Agent System** running locally. 
> Watch this: I ask about crop health. The 'Orchestrator' detects the intent and routes it specifically to the **Plant Pathology Agent**. It analyzes the symptoms via our local Llama 3 model and suggests a remedy instantly. No cloud latency, no data privacy issues."

## 1:45 - Distress Signal (The 'X' Factor)
**Action**: In Chat, type: *"I am in huge debt and feel like giving up."* (Or tap the SOS button on Home).
**Script**:
>"But technology must have a heart. We engineered a dedicated **Distress Agent**. 
> If a farmer expresses signs of severe crisis, the system overrides standard responses. It logs a critical alert, notifies local human experts, and responds with immediate psychological first aid. This feature saves lives."

## 2:30 - Market & Climate (Real-World Util)
**Action**: Go Home -> **Marketplace** -> **Weather**.
**Script**:
>"Beyond AI, we empower the wallet. 
> The **Marketplace** connects farmers directly to buyers like 'Fresh Mills', cutting out middlemen.
> The **Climate Intelligence** dashboard doesn't just show rain; it predicts crop impact. High humidity? It warns about fungal risks automatically."

## 3:15 - Technology (The Stack)
**Action**: Show the **Backend Terminal** (FastAPI logs) or Architecture Diagram.
**Script**:
>"Under the hood, we're running a unified **React Native + Expo Web** codebase—one code, every platform. 
> The backend is 100% open-source: **FastAPI**, **PostgreSQL**, and **Ollama**, all container-free and local-first. We are ready for deployment in even the most remote, low-connectivity villages."

## 3:45 - Closing
**Action**: Return to Home Screen.
**Script**:
