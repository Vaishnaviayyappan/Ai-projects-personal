# 🎤 Farm Connect - Hackathon Pitch Script

**Time Limit**: 2 Minutes
**Target Audience**: Judges, Investors, Technical Reviewers

---

## 0:00 - The Hook (Problem)
*(Show Landing Page with Dark/Professional Aesthetic)*

"Judges, agriculture is a $5 trillion industry, yet it's running on 2G technology. A farmer in Punjab sells his wheat for ₹20/kg, while the end consumer pays ₹300. The middleman takes the rest.
Furthermore, climate change is destroying 30% of yields because farmers lack real-time data.
Existing apps are fragmented. You need one for weather, one for selling, one for advice.
**This ends today.**"

## 0:30 - The Solution (Product Reveal)
*(Scroll down to the 'Operating System' hero section)*

"Introducing **Farm Connect**.
It’s not just an app; it’s the **Operating System for Precision Agriculture**.
We’ve built a unified platform that combines **Marketplace**, **AI Intelligence**, and **Community** into a single, offline-first codebase."

## 0:50 - The Demo (Key Features)

### 1. Dynamic Dashboard
*(Navigate to Dashboard/Weather)*
"Look at this dashboard. It’s not static.
Our **Weather Agent** is pulling live hyper-local data from open APIs. If rain is predicted in 2 hours, the farmer gets a 'Halt Spraying' alert instantly."

### 2. AI Market Intelligence
*(Navigate to Marketplace)*
"Clicking on Market. We don't just show prices. Our **Market Agent** analyzes historical trends.
See this widget? It says 'Wheat +12%'. The AI advises the farmer to **HOLD** stock for 2 more days to maximize profit. This immediately puts money in the farmer's pocket."

### 3. Cross-Platform Tech
*(Show toggle between Web view and Mobile view dimensions if possible)*
"Tech-wise, we achieved the holy grail: **One Codebase, Everywhere.**
This exact React Native code runs as a blazing fast Web App and a native Android app. No duplicate teams. 50% less cost."

## 1:30 - The 'Wow' Factor (Agents)
*(Navigate to Chat/Expert)*
"But the real magic is under the hood. We use a **Multi-Agent Orchestrator**.
If a farmer types 'I have yellow rust', the system doesn't just chat. It:
1.  Routes to the **Crop Doctor Agent**.
2.  Diagnoses the issue.
3.  **Automatically** checks the Marketplace for the correct fungicide.
4.  Adds it to their cart.
It turns advice into action."

## 1:50 - Conclusion
*(Return to Landing Page CTA)*

"Farm Connect is 100% open source. It runs locally. It empowers the 500 million smallholder farmers to compete with industrial giants.
We are Farm Connect. **Let’s grow smarter.** Thank you."
